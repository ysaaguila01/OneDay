Our Story

A decade-long journey — from a chance afternoon in Marmaine to the promise we're keeping this December.

April 23, 2016
Where Paths Crossed
Our paths first crossed on a quiet afternoon at Christian's family home in Marmaine. A casual gathering among friends unexpectedly became the beginning of our story.
Soon After
The Beginning
A friend request, a few quiet reactions, and countless late conversations slowly brought us closer together.
June 12, 2016
Officially Us
We officially became partners — the day the adventure truly set sail.
Ten Years Later
Countless Memories
Over the following decade, we shared countless memories, weathered challenges, and learned that the greatest adventures are the ones shared together.
Our Plan
A Quiet Elopement
We once dreamed of a simple elopement, believing an intimate ceremony for just the two of us would be enough.
August 2026
A Different Journey
But a different plan was written for us. With the love and encouragement of our families, our quiet dream grew into a celebration surrounded by everyone who matters most.

Grateful Hearts
