Photos: add your two chosen couple photos to the repo at assets/images/print/cover-couple.jpg (cover) and assets/images/print/story-couple.jpg (Our Story page). Each frame scales the photo down to fit without cropping or stretching it, so portrait or landscape shots both work — until the files exist, the frames simply stay hidden.

Before printing: open Print Preview and confirm — paper size set to A5 Portrait (or A4 with "Fit to Paper" if A5 isn't available), margins set to "None" or "Default" (not "Custom" with extra padding), scale at 100%, and that the QR code lands fully inside a page with no clipping.
